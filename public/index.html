The Village Table

Opening your table...

The Village Table works inside a Zoom meeting. Open it from the Apps menu during your community call.

Sign in to take a seat

To be placed at a table, you need to join this call signed in to your Zoom account, on a computer. Guests and phones cannot be placed.

One quick step

Allow The Village Table to see your name in this meeting so the host can seat you. It does not read your video, audio, or chat.

Find your table

Enter the 5-character code from your GetParentWise form. Your host will place you at a table when the rooms open.

You are on the list. Keep this open or close it. You will be moved to your table when the host opens the rooms.

Your code is remembered on this device only, so you will not need to type it next time.
